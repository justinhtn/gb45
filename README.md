## F1 Race Weather

`f1/` is a weather tracker for the rest of the 2026 Formula 1 season and all of 2027:

- air and estimated track temperature at the race start, rain chance, wind and sky
- a satellite map of each circuit with live rain radar and infrared storm clouds, and a 3D terrain view
- Storm watch: when rain reaches the circuit and which corners get wet first
- a tyre and pit-stop plan worked out from the rain timeline

Live at https://justinhtn.github.io/gb45/f1/

Track outlines in `f1/tracks.json` come from [bacinger/f1-circuits](https://github.com/bacinger/f1-circuits) (MIT, see `f1/TRACKS-LICENSE.md`).
