      Remaining 2026 races

      Forecasts. Races within 16 days use the hourly forecast for the scheduled start. Races further out show what the weather was on the same date over the last three years at the same hour, labelled Typical; they switch to the forecast once it is available.

      Track temperature isn't published in advance, so it is estimated from air temperature and sunshine: track ≈ air + 2 °C + 0.024 °C per W/m² of sunlight. In full sun that puts the asphalt about 20–25 °C above the air; at night races only a couple of degrees.

      Storm watch uses a 15-minute rain forecast for the circuit to time when rain arrives, and the wind about 3 km up (the wind that steers storm cells) for which way it is moving. The rain front is assumed to sweep across the circuit in that direction, so the corners on the upwind side get wet first. Corners are found from the track outline and named by how far into the lap they are, because the outline doesn't carry official turn numbers.

      Map: Esri satellite imagery with 3D terrain from AWS elevation tiles, live rain radar and infrared storm clouds from RainViewer, and yesterday's daylight satellite photo from NASA GIBS. Weather: Open-Meteo. Track outlines: bacinger/f1-circuits (MIT).
