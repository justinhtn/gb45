Remaining 2026 races

How the numbers work. Races within 16 days use the hourly forecast for the scheduled start. Races further out show what the weather was on the same date over the last three years at the same hour, so treat them as typical conditions, not a forecast; they switch to the forecast automatically once it is available. Nobody publishes track temperature in advance, so it is estimated from air temperature and sunshine: track ≈ air + 2 °C + 0.024 °C per W/m² of sunlight. In full sun that puts the asphalt about 20–25 °C above the air, and at night races only a couple of degrees above.

The map shows the latest infrared cloud imagery (cold, tall storm tops show brightest), rain radar where countries publish it, and yesterday's daylight satellite photo. It is live now, so it shows storms approaching the circuit today, not on race day.
